## Description

XBB shows your RuneScape buffs and debuffs as bars with names, countdowns,
and stack counts. Hover over a bar for its full description where available.

Keep everything in one stack or separate buffs and debuffs. Hide effects you
do not want to see, pin others to fixed slots, and reserve those slots while
effects are absent. Optional missing-buff warnings cover upkeep buffs such as
overloads and pocket books, or any buff you choose to watch.

Use category colors and opacity or give individual buffs and debuffs their
own colors. Settings use [PrettyUI](https://mod.io/g/runescape/m/prettyui),
which is a required dependency.

### Getting started

1. In RuneScape, open **Settings > Gameplay > Interfaces > Buff Bar** and turn
   on the effects you want to track. XBB follows the game's visibility filters.
2. Open **Plugins > XB > Open full settings** (hover: **Xijie Buff Bars**),
   or use the Plugin Hub settings icon. The launcher also has quick options
   for separate stacks, replacing the original bars, and locking bar dragging.
   Set category colors and opacity in **Colors**, or individual colors in **Observed**.
3. Right-click a bar to hide, pin, or watch it. Pinned bars can reserve their
   slot while absent. Manage these choices in Hidden, Pins, Missing, and Observed.
4. Hold **Ctrl**, left-click a buff/debuff header or bar, and move the mouse.
   Release **Ctrl** to save its position. **Display > Lock bar dragging** disables
   bar movement only.

Drag the settings window by its title bar. Window and bar positions are saved
between sessions.

It does not use HTTP, chat, screenshots, or file I/O. Local settings are saved
through the client's `PersistentDB` API.

### Data Protection (case 1)

#### Data used

XBB reads the local player's visible buff and debuff state, including effect
identities, names, descriptions, counts, progress, and timer text. It also reads
the local player's equipped pocket-slot item, active pocket-book timer, and
selected quiver ammunition to label those effects and display available timing.

Interface size and scale, local mouse position, Ctrl-key state, and XBB's
hover/click events are used to lay out and operate the interface. Client logic
ticks drive countdowns. No information about other players is collected.

#### Transmission and storage

This information is not transmitted outside the plugin under any circumstances.
XBB has no network endpoints, telemetry, or external services.

User preferences, positions, dimensions, category colors, individual RGBA color
overrides, hidden effects, pins, and missing-buff rules are stored locally
between sessions through `PersistentDB`. Observed
effect identities, original and compact names, categories, hover descriptions,
and learned durations are also retained there for effect controls and estimated
initial countdowns.

Errors and bounded diagnostic details are written to the local client console.
These can include effect identifiers, visible labels, component types, and
available effect metadata. XBB does not transmit these logs.
